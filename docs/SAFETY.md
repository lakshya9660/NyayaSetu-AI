# NyayaSetu AI — Safety & Legal Compliance Guidelines

## 1. Non-Lawyer Disclaimer Policy

Under the **Advocates Act, 1961**, only qualified advocates registered with a State Bar Council are licensed to provide legal advice and represent clients in court.

NyayaSetu AI strictly abides by the following rules:
- **No Attorney-Client Relationship:** Using this platform does not constitute legal counsel or representation.
- **No Case Outcome Predictions:** The assistant never predicts chances of winning or promises judicial results.
- **No Personalized Strategic Advice:** Explanations focus on explaining statutory provisions in plain English and Hindi, not directing individual litigation tactics.
- **Prominent Disclaimers:** Every page, chat response, and footer explicitly displays the non-lawyer notice.

## 2. Prohibition of Sensitive Personal Identifiers

To prevent data misuse and identity theft:
- NyayaSetu **never requests or stores** Aadhaar numbers, biometric data, PAN cards, or private case records.
- Inquiries should remain general (e.g. "What are the rights of a tenant?" rather than "Here is my private landlord dispute lease file").

## 3. Grounded Sources & Anti-Hallucination Controls

- Every statutory claim must cite the approved document title, page number, and section.
- If a query cannot be verified from approved records, the system triggers the **insufficient-evidence fallback**, directing the citizen to:
  - **NALSA Portal:** https://nalsa.gov.in/legal-aid/
  - **National Legal Aid Helpline:** 15100 (Toll-Free)
  - **Tele-Law Portal:** https://www.tele-law.in/
  - **District Court DLSA Front Offices**
